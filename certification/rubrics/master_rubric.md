# Master Grading Rubric

## A. Technical correctness — 25%
- 5: correct, precise, handles uncertainty
- 4: mostly correct, minor omissions
- 3: adequate but shallow
- 2: important gaps
- 1: materially wrong
- 0: unsafe or unusable

## B. Evidence discipline — 20%
- Separates observed evidence from hypothesis.
- Uses live tools for current state.
- Does not fabricate device output.

## C. Architecture quality — 15%
- Clear boundaries among model, retrieval, tools, deterministic logic, policy, and human approval.
- Avoids unnecessary multi-agent complexity.

## D. Evaluation quality — 15%
- Baseline exists.
- Held-out test exists.
- Metrics match the task.
- Regressions are analyzed.

## E. Safety / governance — 15%
- Least privilege.
- Injection handling.
- Approval gates.
- Rollback / failure handling.
- Auditability.

## F. Communication — 10%
- Clear diagrams.
- Concise rationale.
- Limitations stated explicitly.
