# Lab 6 — Preference and Verifiable Reward Design

**Recommended time:** 2.5 h  
**Points:** 20

Create:
- 10 chosen/rejected troubleshooting pairs,
- 5 verifiable networking tasks,
- at least one deterministic reward function.

Explain:
- which behavior belongs in DPO/preference optimization,
- which task is appropriate for RLVR,
- how the reward could be gamed.

**Deliverable:** preference JSONL + reward code + short risk analysis.


## Grading dimensions

- technical correctness,
- reproducibility,
- evidence discipline,
- evaluation quality,
- safety,
- clarity.
