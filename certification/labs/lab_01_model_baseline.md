# Lab 1 — Model Baseline and Prompt Engineering

**Recommended time:** 2 h  
**Points:** 15

Choose five networking prompts across routing, switching, troubleshooting, and operations.

Compare:
1. weak prompt,
2. structured prompt,
3. structured prompt + few-shot example.

Measure:
- correctness,
- format adherence,
- unsupported claims,
- latency.

**Deliverable:** 1-page findings table plus the exact prompts used.

**Key learning:** Fine-tuning is not justified until a strong prompt baseline exists.


## Grading dimensions

- technical correctness,
- reproducibility,
- evidence discipline,
- evaluation quality,
- safety,
- clarity.
