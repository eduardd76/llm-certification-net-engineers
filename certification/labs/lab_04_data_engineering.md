# Lab 4 — Build a Network SFT Dataset

**Recommended time:** 2.5 h  
**Points:** 20

Create 30 expert-quality SFT examples.

Minimum coverage:
- 5 OSPF/BGP,
- 5 EVPN/VXLAN,
- 5 VLAN/STP/physical,
- 5 MTU/DNS/IPv6,
- 5 incident-format examples,
- 5 safety/counterexamples.

Run:
- duplicate check,
- train/eval leakage check,
- task balance check,
- provenance review.

**Deliverable:** JSONL + dataset card + quality report.


## Grading dimensions

- technical correctness,
- reproducibility,
- evidence discipline,
- evaluation quality,
- safety,
- clarity.
