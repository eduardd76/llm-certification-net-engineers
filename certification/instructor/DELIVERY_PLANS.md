# Delivery Plans

## Option A — 8 full training days

| Day | Focus | Approx. hours |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Foundations: AI -> transformers -> tokens -> model lifecycle | 7 |
| 2 | Prompting, embeddings, RAG, GraphRAG | 7 |
| 3 | Data engineering, SFT, LoRA | 7 |
| 4 | QLoRA, evaluation, Practical Exam 1 | 7 |
| 5 | Preferences, DPO, GRPO/RLVR, distillation | 7 |
| 6 | Tools, agents, MCP/A2A | 7 |
| 7 | AI NOC architecture, digital twin, serving, governance | 7 |
| 8 | Practical Exam 2 + capstone presentations | 7 |

Total: ~56 hours.

## Option B — 12 half-day sessions

1. AI/ML/LLM foundations
2. Transformers and inference
3. Prompting and structured outputs
4. Embeddings/RAG
5. GraphRAG + architecture assignment
6. Data engineering
7. SFT/LoRA/QLoRA
8. Evaluation + Practical Exam 1
9. Preferences/DPO/RLVR
10. Tools/agents/MCP/A2A
11. Production AI NOC + digital twin
12. Practical Exam 2 + capstone defense

## Instructor cadence

Every session should contain:
- 20–30 min concept explanation,
- live demonstration,
- hands-on lab,
- debrief,
- short quiz or design defense.

Avoid teaching the course as a sequence of slide lectures.
